# Traffic Manager gameplay

1. **Read the demand.** Each scenario supplies fixed street and property connections. Hold a connection to see its scheduled destinations and trip counts, even on a cleared map.
2. **Lay out the network.** Choose a lane preset and tap two endpoints for a straight road. To curve it as you draw, hold the destination until the ball appears, then drag sideways. The destination stays where you first touched. You can also drag directly from an existing node for a straight road. Use **Add node** to split a road, or **Clear map** to start with the permanent connections. Undo and redo apply to individual edits.
3. **Design around the site.** The school, houses, fields, and trees occupy real space. Road width and curvature count when checking whether a road fits. Crossing roads automatically split at a shared junction.
4. **Refine the geometry.** Drag a junction node in **Select** to move all its attached roads. Use the curve handles to reshape a segment. At a lane drop, leave the extra lane to taper or start a slip lane from its green handle in **Draw**. Tap the destination junction for a close-up of its receiving lanes. Select a one-way slip segment and use **Move lane start** or **Move lane end** to reattach it to another lane. Grid, angle, and road snaps are available while drawing and moving nodes.
5. **Set the traffic rules.** Open **Intersections** and tap a junction. Draw from a blue incoming lane to a purple outgoing lane to allow a movement. Erase its arrow to forbid it. **Signal** lets SUMO time the allowed movements. Select individual arrows to mix **Stop** and **Yield** at an unsignalized junction.
6. **Run and revise.** Compare the design with the original using the same trip schedule. Play, scrub, or jump to **Worst backup** to see where traffic queues. The results report compares completed trips, delays, queues, and collisions. Disconnected trips count as unserved.

School Pickup includes north, south, west, and east street connections, school access, and the pickup loop. Each location uses one connection for incoming and outgoing demand. Existing corridor saves open with their original controls. The menu offers **Try new Road Builder** to load the editable starter layout as a draft.
