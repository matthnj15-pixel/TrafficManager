# Traffic Manager gameplay

1. **Read the demand.** Each scenario supplies fixed street and property connections. Hold a connection to see its scheduled destinations and trip counts, even on a cleared map.
2. **Lay out the network.** In **Draw**, choose a lane layout and a road shape. **Straight** uses two points. **Curve** uses two points and an adjustable end angle. **Complex** passes through a tapped break point, with handles at the break and the end. In **Freeform**, tap to extend the road and hold and drag a span to bend it. The curve ball marks a held gesture. Tap **Apply** to build the staged road. Toggle road, angle, grid, and node snapping independently.
3. **Design around the site.** The school, houses, fields, and trees occupy real space. Road width and curvature count when checking whether a road fits. Crossing roads automatically split at a shared junction.
4. **Refine the geometry.** In **Select**, tap a road for lane, curve, and alignment controls. **Move segment** slides the lanes sideways one lane width at a time while the junctions stay in place. Drag a junction to move all its roads. In **Draw**, use an extra lane’s green handle to start a slip lane, or leave it to taper. Tap the destination junction for a close-up of its receiving lanes. Select a one-way slip segment and use **More tools** to reattach either end. The eraser enters tap-to-delete mode. A second tap on the eraser clears all roads, and **Undo** restores them.
5. **Set the traffic rules.** In **Select**, tap a junction to edit it. Set a signal, all-way stop, or all-way yield for the whole intersection, or select a blue incoming lane and apply Stop or Yield to that lane. In **Allowed movements**, connect a blue incoming lane to a purple outgoing lane to permit a turn. **Remove all** clears the movements, and **Reset default** restores the automatic movements.
6. **Run and revise.** Compare the design with the original using the same trip schedule. Play, scrub, or jump to **Worst backup** to see where traffic queues. The results report compares completed trips, delays, queues, and collisions. Disconnected trips count as unserved.

School Pickup includes north, south, west, and east street connections, school access, and the pickup loop. Each location uses one connection for incoming and outgoing demand. Existing corridor saves open with their original controls. The menu offers **Try new Road Builder** to load the editable starter layout as a draft.
