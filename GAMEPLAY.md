# Traffic Manager gameplay

1. **Choose a level.** Each level starts with a traffic problem. Use **Move** to clear the active tool and pan or zoom from anywhere. In **Draw** or **Select**, hold a named connection to see its scheduled destinations and trip counts.
2. **Lay out the network.** In **Draw**, choose a lane layout and a road shape. **Straight** uses two points. **Curve** uses two points and an adjustable end angle. **Complex** passes through a tapped break point, with handles at the break and the end. In **Freeform**, tap to extend the road and hold and drag a span to bend it. The curve ball marks a held gesture. Tap **Apply** to build the staged road. Toggle road, angle, grid, and node snapping independently.
3. **Design around the site.** Buildings, fields, and trees occupy real space. The builder checks road width and curvature against the available space. Crossing roads at the same elevation automatically split at a shared junction. Choose **Overpass** or **Underpass** in Draw to cross another road without a junction. Leave room for the ramps. To change a built segment’s elevation, select it and open **More tools**.
4. **Refine the geometry.** In **Select**, tap a road and choose **Adjust curve**. Slide the round entry and exit handles to change the bend while keeping the headings. On a lane-attached curve with an editable join, drag its square along the host road to move the connection. **Move segment** slides the lanes sideways one lane width at a time. Use an extra lane’s green handle to start a slip lane, or leave it to taper. Tap a destination junction to pick its receiving lane. Select a one-way slip segment and open **More tools** to reattach either end. Tap the eraser once to delete a road by tapping it, or tap the eraser again to clear the map. **Undo** restores the edit.
5. **Set the traffic rules.** In **Select**, tap a junction to edit it. Set a signal, all-way stop, or all-way yield for the whole intersection, or select a blue incoming lane and apply Stop or Yield to that lane. In **Allowed movements**, connect a blue incoming lane to a purple outgoing lane to permit a turn. **Remove all** clears the movements, and **Reset default** restores the automatic movements.
6. **Run and revise.** Compare the design with the original using the same trip schedule. Play, scrub, or jump to **Worst backup** to see where traffic queues. The results report compares completed trips, delays, queues, and collisions. Disconnected trips count as unserved.

At eligible intersections, **Build structure** offers three roundabout sizes. Each creates a single counterclockwise lane, and approaching traffic yields to vehicles in the circle. At a four-way crossing, choose an axis and build an **Overpass** or **Underpass** to give the two through routes independent paths.

School Pickup includes north, south, west, and east street connections, school access, and the pickup loop. Each location uses one connection for incoming and outgoing demand. Existing corridor saves open with their original controls. The menu offers **Try new Road Builder** to load the editable starter layout as a draft.
