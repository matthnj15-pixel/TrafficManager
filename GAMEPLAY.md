# Traffic Manager gameplay

1. **Choose a level.** Each level starts with a traffic problem. Use **Move** to clear the active tool and pan or zoom from anywhere. In **Draw** or **Select**, hold a named connection to see its scheduled destinations and trip counts.
2. **Lay out the network.** In **Draw**, **Auto** starts a lane connector when you tap a road. Follow the direction arrows to choose a departure lane and then a receiving lane. If lanes overlap, choose the direction and lane in the magnified picker. Tap open ground to lay an ordinary road. **Straight** uses two points. **Curve** uses two points and an adjustable end angle. **Complex** passes through a tapped break point, with handles at the break and the end. In **Freeform**, tap to extend the road and hold and drag a span to bend it. The curve ball marks a held gesture. For a lane connector, preview **Direct** or **Loop**, or hold and pull the destination to change the bend. Check the crossings and clearance before tapping **Apply**. Apply inserts the fork, merge, and connector together. **Cancel** discards the preview. Toggle road, angle, grid, and node snapping independently.
3. **Design around the site.** Buildings, fields, and trees occupy real space. The builder checks road width and curvature against the available space. Crossing roads at the same elevation automatically split at a shared junction. Choose **Overpass** or **Underpass** in Draw to cross another road without a junction. Leave room for the ramps. To change a built segment’s elevation, select it and open **More tools**.
4. **Refine the geometry.** Use **Move** on an Auto connector to slide its departure or merge along the host, change its lane, or drag the curve ball. Tap **Apply** to keep the edit. To widen the road between a merge and an exit, select that individual segment and add a lane on the desired side. In **Select**, tap a manual road and choose **Adjust curve**. Slide the round entry and exit handles to change the bend while keeping the headings. On a lane-attached curve with an editable join, drag its square along the host road to move the connection. **Move segment** slides the lanes sideways one lane width at a time. Use an extra lane’s green handle to start a slip lane, or leave it to taper. Tap a destination junction to pick its receiving lane. Select a one-way slip segment and open **More tools** to reattach either end. Tap the eraser once to delete a road by tapping it, or tap the eraser again to clear the map. **Undo** restores the edit.
5. **Set the traffic rules.** In **Select**, tap a junction to edit it. Set a signal, all-way stop, or all-way yield for the whole intersection, or select a blue incoming lane and apply Stop or Yield to that lane. In **Allowed movements**, connect a blue incoming lane to a purple outgoing lane to permit a turn. **Remove all** clears the movements, and **Reset default** restores the automatic movements.
6. **Run and revise.** Compare the design with the original using the same trip schedule. Play, scrub, or jump to **Worst backup** to see where traffic queues. The results report compares completed trips, delays, queues, and collisions. The movement report distinguishes scheduled demand with a route from trips SUMO actually completed. Disconnected trips count as unserved. The weave highlight marks the stretch between a merge and the next exit so you can follow approaching vehicles during replay.

At eligible intersections, **Build structure** offers three roundabout sizes. Each creates a single counterclockwise lane, and approaching traffic yields to vehicles in the circle. At a four-way crossing choose **Select → Build structure**, then choose the pair of through arms and **Surface**, **Over**, or **Under**. Review the highlighted arms, affected turns, and scheduled demand before applying a separation. The two through paths stay connected across the site. Turning between them requires another junction or a ramp.

School Pickup includes north, south, west, and east street connections, school access, and the pickup loop. Each location uses one connection for incoming and outgoing demand. Existing corridor saves open with their original controls. The menu offers **Try new Road Builder** to load the editable starter layout as a draft.
